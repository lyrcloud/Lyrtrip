# Google Play listing — Lyrtrip

Everything to paste into Play Console. Files are in this folder.

## Main store listing

**App name** (30 max): `Lyrtrip`

**Short description** (80 max):
`Book flights, hotels, parcels, freight, tours and more, all in one travel app.`

**Full description** (4000 max):

```
Lyrtrip is one app for getting yourself, your family and your things anywhere in the world. Book flights, request hotels, ship parcels, move freight, plan tours and more, with prices in your own currency.

FLIGHTS AND AIR CHARTERS
• Search and book flights on hundreds of airlines worldwide
• Request a helicopter, seaplane or air ambulance charter and get a quote

STAYS
• Request hotels, resorts, homestays, apartments, villas and more
• Our team confirms your booking and sends the details by email

RIDES
• Compare ride prices for cars, taxis, limos and motorcycles on your route

SHIPPING AND COURIER
• Send parcels anywhere with live rates from USPS, FedEx, UPS, DHL and more
• Same-day local courier delivery
• Book a truck for moving or haulage, or ship cargo and containers by sea, air or rail

TOURS AND TRAVEL SERVICES
• Tour packages, family holidays, honeymoons, pilgrimages and educational tours
• Help with visas, passports, ID cards and travel insurance

STUDY AND JOBS
• Apply to study abroad, with document uploads and application tracking
• Create a job profile so companies can find you, and apply for jobs

TAX, LEGAL AND LAWYERS
• Request tax returns, GST/VAT help, legal documents, registrations and lawyer advice

EVENTS AND CHARITY
• Request tickets for concerts, shows and summits
• Donate to causes you care about

REWARDS
• Earn points on bookings and redeem them later
• Buy gift cards for friends and family

PAY YOUR WAY
• Prices in your local currency and language
• Secure card payments through Stripe
• Track every booking and request in your dashboard

LYRBOT ASSISTANT
• Ask Lyrbot for help with any booking, any time

Questions? Email support@lyrtrip.com.
```
